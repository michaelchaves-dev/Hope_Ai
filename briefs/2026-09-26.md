### 1. Belzutifan + lenvatinib approved for advanced ccRCC — `approved`

- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Source:** FDA Drugs@FDA announcement
- **Fact:** FDA approved belzutifan (Welireg, Merck) with lenvatinib (Lenvima, Eisai) for adults with advanced renal cell carcinoma with a clear cell component after PD-1/PD-L1 inhibitor therapy. Efficacy from LITESPARK-011 (NCT04586231, n=747): median PFS 14.6 vs 10.6 months vs cabozantinib (HR 0.74); OS not statistically significant at final analysis; ORR 53% vs 40%.
- **URL:** https://www.fda.gov/drugs/resources-information-approved-drugs/fda-approves-belzutifan-combination-lenvatinib-advanced-renal-cell-carcinoma-clear-cell-component
